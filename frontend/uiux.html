UI/UX Design Challenge

Create intuitive, attractive, and user-friendly digital experiences by solving real-world design problems through creativity and innovation.

About the Event

The UI/UX Design Challenge is a creative competition where participants design engaging digital interfaces and meaningful user experiences. Participants will work on a given problem statement and develop a complete design solution.

Objective

The main objective is to encourage participants to understand user needs, apply design thinking, and create simple, accessible, and visually appealing interfaces.

Event Information

👥 Participants Individual or Team
⏱ Duration 2–3 Hours
📍 Mode On-site
🎯 Level Beginner to Advanced

Challenge Format

  • A real-world design problem will be provided.
  • Participants identify the target users and their needs.
  • Create wireframes and user flows.
  • Develop a high-fidelity UI design.
  • Present the final design to the judges.

Design Tools

  • Figma
  • Adobe XD
  • Canva
  • Sketch
  • Other UI/UX design tools

Judging Criteria

  • User experience and usability
  • Creativity and innovation
  • Visual design quality
  • Problem-solving approach
  • Consistency and accessibility
  • Presentation quality

Rules & Guidelines

  • Participants must follow the given problem statement.
  • Designs should be original and creative.
  • Ready-made templates should be avoided.
  • Participants must submit their design within the given time.
  • Final design must be presented to the judging panel.

What You Will Learn

  • User research and problem identification
  • Wireframing and prototyping
  • Visual hierarchy and design principles
  • Responsive interface design
  • Design thinking and usability testing

Prizes & Recognition

Outstanding designs will receive prizes, certificates, recognition, and an opportunity to showcase their work.
