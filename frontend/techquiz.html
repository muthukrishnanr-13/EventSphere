Tech Quiz

Test your technical knowledge, challenge your thinking, and compete with other participants through an exciting technology-based quiz.

About the Event

Tech Quiz is an engaging technical competition designed to test participants' knowledge of programming, web development, databases, cloud computing, artificial intelligence, and emerging technologies.

Objective

The objective of the event is to improve technical awareness, encourage continuous learning, and provide participants with an opportunity to test their knowledge in a competitive environment.

Event Information

👥 Participation Individual / Team
⏱ Duration 30–45 Minutes
📍 Mode On-site / Online
🎯 Level Beginner to Advanced

Quiz Format

  • Multiple-choice technical questions.
  • Questions covering different technology domains.
  • Programming and computer science fundamentals.
  • Web, database, cloud, and AI-related questions.
  • Time-based rounds may be included.
  • Highest-scoring participants will qualify for the final round.

Topics Covered

  • Programming and Data Structures
  • Web Development
  • Database Management
  • Cloud Computing
  • Artificial Intelligence
  • Cyber Security
  • Computer Fundamentals
  • Emerging Technologies

Judging Criteria

  • Number of correct answers
  • Accuracy
  • Time taken to answer
  • Final round performance

Rules & Guidelines

  • Participants must follow the given time limit.
  • Each question should be answered within the allotted time.
  • Use of unauthorized external assistance is not allowed.
  • Participants must follow the instructions of the organizers.
  • Organizer decisions will be final.

What You Will Learn

  • Improved technical knowledge
  • Better understanding of computer science concepts
  • Awareness of emerging technologies
  • Faster analytical thinking
  • Competitive problem-solving skills

Prizes & Recognition

Top-performing participants will receive recognition and certificates. Winners may also receive exciting prizes based on the final event evaluation.
