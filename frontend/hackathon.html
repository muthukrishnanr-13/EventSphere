TECHNICAL EVENT

Hackathon

Collaborate, innovate and build real-world solutions with your team. Put your technical knowledge into practice and turn your ideas into working solutions.

01. About the Hackathon

The Hackathon is an intensive technical event where participants work individually or in teams to develop innovative solutions for real-world problems.


Participants are encouraged to identify a problem, design a solution, develop a working prototype and present their idea to the judging panel.

02. Objective

The main objective is to encourage creativity, teamwork, problem-solving and practical application of programming and technology skills.

03. Event Information

  • 👥 Team Size: 2 – 4 Members
  • ⏱️ Duration: 24 Hours
  • 💻 Mode: On-site
  • 🎯 Level: Beginner to Advanced
  • 🏆 Category: Innovation & Technology

04. Technologies

Participants can choose suitable technologies based on their project requirements.


  • Frontend: HTML, CSS, JavaScript
  • Backend: Node.js / Python
  • Database: MongoDB / MySQL
  • Other: AI, Cloud, APIs

05. Judging Criteria

  • Innovation and originality
  • Technical implementation
  • Problem-solving approach
  • User experience and design
  • Project presentation

06. Rules & Guidelines

  1. Participants must register before the event begins.
  2. Teams should consist of 2 to 4 members.
  3. Projects must be developed during the hackathon.
  4. Participants should explain their project clearly during the final presentation.
  5. All team members should contribute to the project.

07. Prizes & Recognition

The best-performing teams will receive recognition based on innovation, implementation and presentation quality.


  • 🥇 First Prize
  • 🥈 Second Prize
  • 🥉 Third Prize
  • 📜 Participation Certificate

08. What You Will Learn

  • Team collaboration
  • Real-world problem solving
  • Project development
  • Presentation skills
  • Innovation and creativity
