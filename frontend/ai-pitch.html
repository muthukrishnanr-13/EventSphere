INNOVATION EVENT

AI Idea Pitch

Present innovative artificial intelligence ideas, solve real-world problems, and turn creative concepts into meaningful technology solutions.

About the Event

AI Idea Pitch is an innovation-focused event where participants present creative ideas using Artificial Intelligence. Participants are encouraged to identify real-world problems and propose practical AI-powered solutions.

Objective

The objective is to encourage innovation, creativity, problem-solving, and the practical use of Artificial Intelligence to address real-world challenges.

Event Information

👥 Participation Individual / Team
⏱ Duration 5–10 Minutes per Pitch
📍 Mode On-site / Online
🎯 Level Beginner to Advanced

Pitch Format

  • Introduce the problem you want to solve.
  • Explain your proposed AI-based solution.
  • Describe the target users and use case.
  • Explain the technologies required.
  • Present the expected impact of the solution.
  • Answer questions from the judges.

Technologies

  • Artificial Intelligence
  • Machine Learning
  • Generative AI
  • Python
  • Cloud Services
  • APIs and Web Technologies

Judging Criteria

  • Innovation and originality
  • Problem relevance
  • Technical feasibility
  • Potential real-world impact
  • Presentation quality
  • Clarity of the proposed solution

Rules & Guidelines

  • Ideas must be original and relevant.
  • Participants must stay within the given time limit.
  • The proposed solution should be technically feasible.
  • Participants should clearly explain their idea.
  • Organizer decisions will be final.

What You Will Learn

  • AI problem-solving techniques
  • Idea development and innovation
  • Technical presentation skills
  • Understanding real-world AI applications
  • Teamwork and communication

Prizes & Recognition

Outstanding ideas and presentations will receive recognition and certificates. Winners may also receive exciting prizes based on the final evaluation.
